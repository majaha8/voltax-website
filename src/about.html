About Volt-Watt

A certified electrical and infrastructure solutions company serving South Africa since 2025.

Our Story

Built on excellence

Founded in 2025, Volt-Watt started as a small electrical contracting outfit with a simple goal: deliver electrical work South African homeowners and businesses could actually trust. Over the years we've grown into a full-service infrastructure company trusted by hundreds of clients across the country.

We partner with public and private sector clients to solve electrical challenges with a combination of proven technique and deep industry experience. Our certified electricians, engineers and project managers work to make sure every job is delivered on time, on budget, and to the standard it should be.
