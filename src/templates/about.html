About Volt-Watt

A certified electrical and infrastructure solutions company serving South Africa .

Our Story

Built on excellence

Volt-Watt started as a small electrical contracting outfit with a simple goal: deliver electrical work South African homeowners and businesses could actually trust. Over the years we've grown into a full-service infrastructure company trusted by clients across the country.

We partner with public and private sector clients to solve electrical challenges with a combination of proven technique and deep industry experience. Our certified electricians, engineers and project managers work to make sure every job is delivered on time, on budget, and to the standard it should be.

  • SANS-compliant work with certificates of compliance issued on request
  • One accountable team from first quote through to sign-off
  • Certified electricians, engineers and project managers
